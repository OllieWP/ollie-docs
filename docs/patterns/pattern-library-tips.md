Once you know your way around the Ollie Pattern Library, a few power features make it dramatically faster to go from blank page to finished design. Here are the three biggest, each with a quick video.

Every pattern renders as a live, interactive preview — real blocks, your theme's styles, animations and all — not a static screenshot. Open a pattern and use the **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** toggles to see exactly how it responds before you commit. What you preview is what you get, because it's already running on your site's design system.

Found patterns you love? Click **Add to Favorites** and they join your **My Favorites** collection — one click away every time you open the library. Favorites are saved site-wide, so the shortlist you curate while planning is right there during every build session. Most sites settle into a working set of 10–15 go-to patterns; favorites make that set instant.

## Search, Filter, Sort, and Edit

The library is built for finding things fast:

- **Search** patterns by name, and browse by **Categories** and **Collections** in the sidebar.
- **Filter** to **My Favorites** or **Downloaded to theme**, and **Sort** by latest or name.
- Switch the **Grid View** between 2, 3, and 4 columns to scan more at once.
- Insert patterns as **static** (a clean starting design) or **dynamic** — dynamic patterns pull real content from your site, like your actual latest posts.
- Use **Bulk Edit** to insert or download several patterns at once, and **Add to theme** to save a pattern into your theme for offline, permanent use.
