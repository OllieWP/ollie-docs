Ollie ships complete WooCommerce support: a full set of block-built store templates, professionally designed shop patterns, one-click product card layouts, and all of Ollie Pro's design tools working seamlessly with your products. Build a modern, blazing-fast store in the native block editor — no page builder, no code.

## Getting Set Up

There's nothing to configure. With the Ollie theme active, install and activate **WooCommerce** and the store templates, styles, and patterns light up automatically. Until WooCommerce is active, Ollie keeps all of its shop templates and patterns tucked away so they never clutter a non-store site.

For the fastest start, the **Setup Wizard** in the Ollie dashboard can handle plugin installation, page creation, and styling in one pass — or import the **eCommerce starter site** for a complete, ready-to-customize store.

## Store Templates

Every WooCommerce page on your site is a real block template, fully editable in the Site Editor with the same tools you use everywhere else:

- **Shop / Product Archive** — your main catalog page, with a sidebar variation included.
- **Single Product** — the product page, built from WooCommerce blocks you can rearrange and restyle freely.
- **Cart** and **Checkout** — clean, conversion-focused, and fully editable.
- **Order Confirmation** — a designed thank-you moment instead of a plain receipt.
- **Product Search Results** — search that matches the rest of your store.
- **Coming Soon** — a polished holding page while you build.

Ollie also replaces WooCommerce's stock block patterns with its own curated set, so everything you insert matches your store's design system — product imagery gets consistent, tidy cropping out of the box.

## Product Card Layouts (Ollie Pro)

The fastest way to restyle your whole catalog: select a **Product Collection** (or Product Template) block and open the **Product Layouts** panel in the sidebar. Click any of the five **Product Card** designs — centered cards, cards with star ratings, hover add-to-cart overlays, pill-button treatments — and the entire product grid switches to that layout instantly.

It swaps the layout of every card in the collection in one click, and the editor's undo brings back the previous design if you change your mind. Themes and developers can add their own designs to the panel too: any pattern registered in the `ollie/product-card` category appears there automatically.

## Designing the Rest of Your Store

- **Woo Header** — a store-ready header pattern with cart integration, ready to customize in the Site Editor.
- **Mega menus** — use [Menu Designer](/docs/extensions/menu-designer/) to build shop navigation with product grids, featured items, and promo cards right in the dropdown.
- **WooCommerce pattern collection** — Ollie Pro's pattern library includes a dedicated WooCommerce collection: featured product sections, promotional banners, pricing tables, hero headers, and video cards, all drop-in ready.
- **Pro extensions everywhere** — everything in the Extensions suite works on store content: [Animation Designer](/docs/extensions/animation-designer/) for product reveals, [Advanced Grid Controls](/docs/extensions/advanced-grid/) for responsive catalog grids, [Smart Sync](/docs/extensions/smart-sync/) to restyle repeating product sections, and [Video Modal](/docs/extensions/video-modal/) for product demo videos.

## Tips

- Restyle first, rebuild never: try all five Product Card layouts on your Shop page before customizing by hand — one of them is usually 90% of the way there.
- The product archive sidebar variation plus WooCommerce's filter blocks gives you a classic filterable catalog with zero custom work.
- A Cover block with a [Video Modal](/docs/extensions/video-modal/) makes a great product-demo section on high-value product pages.
