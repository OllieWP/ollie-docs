## How to Upgrade

{{video id="ME313EkkPc4" title="Upgrade Your Subscription" desc="How to upgrade or manage your Ollie Pro plan."}}

Upgrading your Ollie Pro subscription takes just a few clicks. Here's how to do it:

1. Sign in to your account at [OllieWP.com](https://olliewp.com/signin)
2. Once you're in your account dashboard, look for the "Account Details" panel
3. Find the "Billing and Payments" section and click the "Manage" button
4. You'll be taken to our payment partner, Lemon Squeezy. They'll send you a magic login link via email – no password needed!
5. After logging in, you'll see your Ollie Pro subscription. Click the "…" menu icon in the Subscriptions area
6. Select "Update" from the menu
7. Choose your new plan (Creator, Freelancer, or Agency)
8. Click "Save" to confirm your upgrade

That's it! Your account will be updated right away with your new activation quota. Don't worry about the billing - we'll automatically prorate your upgrade based on your current subscription.

## What Happens Next?

Your upgrade takes effect immediately, so you can start activating Ollie Pro on more sites right away. The additional cost will be prorated based on your current billing cycle, so you'll only pay for the difference.
