## Support for Ollie block theme users

If you are using the free version of the Ollie block theme, you can get help through the WordPress.org forums. Here’s how you can access support:

<!-- wp:list {"className":"is-style-list-boxed","gradient":"light-section-fade"} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list is-style-list-boxed has-light-section-fade-gradient-background has-background"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visit the WordPress.org Forums</strong>:<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Navigate to Ollie's <a href="https://wordpress.org/support/theme/ollie/">WordPress.org forums</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Sign into your WordPress.org account or create one if you don't have one.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Post Your Question</strong>:<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Create a new topic related to the Ollie block theme.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Provide as much detail as possible about your issue to receive more accurate and helpful responses from the Ollie team or the community.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

## For Ollie Pro users

If you are using Ollie Pro, you have access to priority support through your Ollie Pro account. Here’s how to open a priority support ticket:

<!-- wp:list {"className":"is-style-list-boxed","gradient":"light-section-fade"} -->
<ul class="wp-block-list is-style-list-boxed has-light-section-fade-gradient-background has-background"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Log into Your Ollie Pro Account</strong>:<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Go to the <a href="https://olliewp.com/signin/" data-type="page" data-id="57793">Sign In</a> page and enter your credentials (the email you purchased with and the password you created).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Once signed in, navigate to the support section of your Ollie Pro account dashboard towards the bottom of the page.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Open a Priority Support Ticket</strong>:<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Fill out the support form with the necessary details about your issue.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Submit the ticket and a member of the Ollie support team will respond to your inquiry via email as soon as possible.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

## Submit a bug report on Github

If you've spotted a bug in the Ollie block theme, you can [submit an issue](https://github.com/OllieWP/ollie/issues/new/choose)&nbsp;to the&nbsp;[Ollie GitHub repo](https://github.com/OllieWP/ollie/)&nbsp;where we can track and fix the issue for you.
