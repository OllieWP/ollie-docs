## Setup Wizard and Starter Sites

There's nothing to configure by hand. With the Ollie theme active, install and activate **WooCommerce** and the store templates, styles, and patterns light up automatically — and until WooCommerce is active, Ollie keeps its shop templates and patterns tucked away so they never clutter a non-store site.

The **Setup Wizard** in the Ollie dashboard walks you through everything you need to get your store off the ground — installing plugins, creating pages, setting up your branding, generating colors, and more. By the end of it, you've skipped a dozen tedious steps and saved hours.

{{video id="HGLiBGZMOG0" start="40" title="Setup Wizard" desc="Install plugins, create pages, and set up your branding in one pass."}}

Want to move even faster? **Starter Sites** automate the entire setup into a single step: select a starter site, click import, and in seconds your site is completely transformed — new styles, pages, and templates all applied automatically, leaving you a complete store to make your own.

## Header and Navigation

The header is one of the first places to make your own. In the Site Editor, double-click the header to start editing — or jump there instantly from the command palette — then customize your logo, site title, and navigation, or swap in a header design built for WooCommerce, complete with cart integration.

For shop navigation that sells, use [Menu Designer](/docs/design/menu-designer/) to build a content-rich mega menu with your product categories, store info, and featured items — all blocks. Choose from card-style dropdowns, full-width panels, and mobile menus in the Ollie pattern library, and customize to match your brand.

## Store Templates

Every WooCommerce page on your site is a real block template, fully editable in the Site Editor with the same tools you use everywhere else:

- **Shop / Product Archive** — your main catalog page, with a sidebar variation included.
- **Single Product** — the product page, built from WooCommerce blocks you can rearrange and restyle freely.
- **Cart** and **Checkout** — clean, conversion-focused, and fully editable.
- **Order Confirmation** — a designed thank-you moment instead of a plain receipt.
- **Product Search Results** — search that matches the rest of your store.
- **Coming Soon** — a polished holding page while you build.

Because templates are blocks, you have full design control right in the editor: resize the product title, punch up the button, swap in a pattern from the Ollie Pro library — no code. Ollie also replaces WooCommerce's stock block patterns with its own curated set, so everything you insert matches your store's design system.

## Product Collection and Card Layouts

The **Product Collection block** is the engine behind how products display across your store — the main shop grid, featured products, sale items, and hand-picked collections. Add hover effects, or drop in a horizontal-scrolling product carousel for featured items.

With Ollie Pro, restyling your whole catalog takes one click: select a Product Collection (or Product Template) block and open the **Product Layouts** panel in the sidebar. Click any of the five **Product Card** designs — centered cards, star ratings, hover add-to-cart overlays, pill-button treatments — and every card in the grid switches instantly. The editor's undo brings back the previous design if you change your mind, and any pattern registered in the `ollie/product-card` category appears in the panel automatically, so developers can add their own.

## Tips

- Restyle first, rebuild never: try all five Product Card layouts on your Shop page before customizing by hand — one of them is usually 90% of the way there.
- The product archive sidebar variation plus WooCommerce's filter blocks gives you a classic filterable catalog with zero custom work.
- A Cover block with a [Video Modal](/docs/build/video-modal/) makes a great product-demo section on high-value product pages — the same treatment as the video cards in this doc.
