Global Styles is the command center for your site's entire visual language. Define your typography, colors, and spacing once, and those choices cascade through everything — every heading, button, pattern, and template on your site. Change your brand color and watch hundreds of pages update instantly. No CSS, no hunting through pages.

## Where It Lives

In the Site Editor (**Appearance → Editor**), click the **Styles** icon (the half-moon circle) in the top bar. From there you can:

- **Browse styles** — try Ollie's [style variations](/docs/design/style-variations/), complete looks you can apply in one click.
- **Typography** — set the fonts, sizes, and weights for body text, headings, links, and buttons. Ollie includes ten hand-picked typography presets if you'd rather choose a pairing than build one.
- **Colors** — manage your palette. Ollie's palette is a small set of named slots — Primary, Main, Base, and friends — and every block and pattern references those slots rather than hard-coded colors. That's the trick behind everything staying in sync; the [Ollie Color Palette doc](/docs/design/ollie-color-palette/) explains the system.
- **Layout** — content and wide widths, plus site-wide spacing.
- **Blocks** — style a block type once, globally: every Button, every Quote, every Heading level, across the whole site.

## Why This Is a Design System

If you've worked with professional design tools, this is design tokens for WordPress. Your styles are the single source of truth; patterns act like a library of smart components that inherit them automatically. Insert any Ollie pattern and it arrives already matching your brand — because it was built against the token slots, not specific colors and fonts.

{{video id="kC_85zuSnr4" title="WordPress as a Design System" desc="Use full site editing as a no-code design system."}}

The practical payoff: perfect consistency across hundreds of pages, and global redesigns in seconds. Rebrand from blue to green, swap serif for sans, tighten your spacing scale — each is one change in one place.

## A Good Workflow

1. Start from a [style variation](/docs/design/style-variations/) that's close to your vision — Agency, Creator, eCommerce, Startup, or Studio.
2. Adjust the palette slots to your exact brand colors, and pick (or tune) a typography preset.
3. Build with patterns from there — everything you insert will match automatically.
4. Resist per-block color overrides: every time you style through the palette instead of a one-off color, future-you keeps the one-click rebrand superpower.

## Good to Know

- Global Styles has its own revision history — click the clock icon in the Styles panel to step back through changes.
- Style changes are instant and site-wide on save, which is exactly what makes them powerful — preview in the editor before saving.
- [Ollie AI](/docs/ollie-ai/ai-in-the-editor/) works within this system too: AI-generated pages are constrained to your design tokens, so even generated content respects your brand.
